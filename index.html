Approximate rotational reconstruction

This JavaScript clone builds a symmetric approximation from light-background photos of roughly round, upright objects. It does not estimate cameras or run a calibrated visual hull.

For tall, roughly round objects on a light background only. Unseen sides are estimated. Top/bottom labels add surface color; a labeled bottom uses an adjustable estimated recess, not measured 3D relief.
Camera calibration file

Requires calibrated views in Middlebury format, a dark background, and bounds in camera coordinates. Visual hulls cannot recover hidden concavities or fine texture.

OCR is not available in this browser version.
